Create ONE image, 1024 x 1024 pixels, with a fully transparent background.

Divide the image into four equal, INVISIBLE quadrants of 512 x 512 pixels: top-left, top-right, bottom-left and
bottom-right. Draw ONLY in the bottom-left quadrant. The top-left, top-right and bottom-right quadrants must stay
completely empty and fully transparent. Do not draw any grid, divider, border, frame, label, number, letter, arrow or
marker anywhere.

In the bottom-left quadrant, draw the NOSE of the reference character, alone, centred in the quadrant, about 3.5 times as
large as it appears on the reference face: about 80 pixels tall. Scale the line weight with the nose, so the outline looks
as bold relative to the nose as on the reference. Leave at least 60 pixels of empty transparent space between the nose and
the edges of its quadrant. Nothing may touch or cross a quadrant edge.

Draw the nose exactly as on the reference, in the same orientation — do not mirror or flip it:
- a short, slightly curved DARK OUTLINE STROKE on the RIGHT side of the nose;
- a soft, discreet WARM SHADING on the LEFT side of that stroke, only as wide as on the reference;
- the small white highlight just to the RIGHT of the dark stroke, as on the reference.
The nose has NO filled body: do not fill the area between the shading and the highlight, and do not draw any skin-coloured
shape, skin patch or face surface around or behind the nose.

Style — match the reference exactly: the same dark outline colour and weight, the same warm shading tone, the same small
highlight, in the calm neutral default face.

Draw ONLY the nose. Do NOT draw: eyebrows, eyes, eyelids, lashes, folds, mouth, hair, blush, ears, head outline, skin,
shadows cast onto skin, glow, a background colour, text, numbers, grid lines or guide marks.

Edges: clean anti-aliased edges directly on transparency; no halo, no white, grey or skin-coloured fringe.
