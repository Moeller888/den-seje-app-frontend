Create ONE image, 1024 x 1024 pixels, with a fully transparent background.

The image is an asset sheet divided into four equal, INVISIBLE quadrants of 512 x 512 pixels. Do not draw any grid,
divider, border, frame, label, number, letter, arrow or marker anywhere.

Put exactly one facial feature of the reference character in each quadrant:
- top-left quadrant: the eyebrow that appears on the LEFT side of the reference image;
- top-right quadrant: the eyebrow that appears on the RIGHT side of the reference image;
- bottom-left quadrant: the nose;
- bottom-right quadrant: the mouth.

Size and spacing:
- Draw each feature alone, centred in its quadrant, about 3.5 times as large as it appears on the reference face:
  each eyebrow about 300 pixels wide, the nose about 80 pixels tall, the mouth line about 270 pixels wide.
- Scale the line weights with the feature, so the outlines look as bold relative to the feature as on the reference.
- Leave at least 60 pixels of empty transparent space between every feature and the edges of its quadrant.
  Nothing may touch or cross a quadrant edge.

Orientation — keep the reference's exact orientation for every feature; do not straighten, level or rotate anything:
- the mouth line is TILTED: its right end is clearly higher than its left end (about 6 degrees on the reference);
- each eyebrow keeps the reference's angle and curve, with both tips at the same relative heights as on the reference;
- the nose keeps the reference's direction (its short stroke and its small hook as drawn on the reference).

Style — match the reference exactly:
- the same dark outline, the same brown eyebrow fill with its subtle painted texture;
- the same short nose stroke with its small warm shading and small white highlight;
- the same thin dark mouth line with its small end ticks, in the calm neutral default smile.
Both eyebrows are COMPLETE, full length, with natural tapered ends on both sides. Never cut, shorten or hide an
eyebrow, and never shape it around hair.

Draw ONLY these four features. Do NOT draw: skin, a face surface or skin patch, a head outline, eyes, eye whites,
eyelids, lashes, eyelid folds, hair, blush, ears, neck, shadows cast onto skin, glow, a background colour, text, numbers,
grid lines or guide marks.

Edges: opaque feature cores with clean anti-aliased edges directly on transparency; no halo, no white, grey or
skin-coloured fringe.
